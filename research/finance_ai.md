# MSME Credit & Financial Regulation AI

## Objective
To develop models that assist micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) in navigating credit applications, understanding GST regulations, and parsing financial documents.

## Key Datasets Required
- RBI circularly and guidelines.
- SEBI regulations.
- GST rules and FAQs.
- Synthetic financial statements and anonymized credit formats.

## Challenges
- Rapidly changing regulatory environments requiring frequent model updates.
- Necessity for high mathematical accuracy in responses.
- Privacy constraints regarding real financial data.

## Approach
Focus on creating a highly specific Instruct model that excels at tabular data extraction and policy Q&A. We will leverage parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) on base SLMs, combined with a specialized financial calculator tool-use capability.
