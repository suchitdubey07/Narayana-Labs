# Multilingual Education Tutor

## Objective
To create an interactive, patient, and culturally aligned AI tutoring system focused on K-12 education, mapped to the NCERT curriculum and NEP 2020 guidelines.

## Key Datasets Required
- NCERT textbooks (multilingual).
- State board syllabus materials.
- Educational pedagogies and teacher training manuals.
- Educational YouTube transcripts (e.g., Swayamprabha, Diksha).

## Challenges
- Maintaining a pedagogically sound "Socratic" dialogue rather than just giving direct answers.
- Tailoring reading levels to distinct age groups.
- Preventing biases or politically sensitive topics from disrupting the learning experience.

## Approach
We will utilize reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) emphasizing teaching methodologies. The model will be designed to work securely offline via edge computing for use in remote rural schools.
