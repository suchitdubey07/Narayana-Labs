# Narayan Labs
**Open AI Infrastructure for Bharat**

## Overview
Narayan Labs is an open-source AI research organization dedicated to building state-of-the-art Small Language Models (SLMs) tailored for the Indian context. We believe that the future of AI in India lies in efficient, domain-specific, and multilingual models that can run on accessible hardware.

## Mission and Vision
Our mission is to democratize access to AI capabilities across India by building the foundational infrastructure—datasets, models, and tools—that power the next generation of applications. Our vision is a self-reliant AI ecosystem in Bharat that is open, inclusive, and tailored to the unique linguistic and cultural nuances of the subcontinent.

## Focus on Small Language Models (SLMs)
We prioritize SLMs because they offer:
- **Efficiency**: Lower compute requirements for inference.
- **Accessibility**: Ability to run on edge devices and local servers.
- **Domain Specialization**: Better performance on specific tasks compared to generalized massive models.

## Key Research Areas
We are currently focusing on the following vital sectors:
- **Agriculture**: AI advisories for crop management and weather.
- **Law**: Indian legal AI models for accelerating justice.
- **Finance**: MSME credit intelligence and financial regulation assistants.
- **Education**: Multilingual tutors to bridge learning gaps.
- **Public Policy**: Policy interpretation and governance assistants.
