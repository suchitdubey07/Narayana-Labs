# Narayan Labs Fellowship Program

The Narayan Labs Fellowship is a highly competitive, 6-month program aimed at India’s brightest engineers, data scientists, and domain experts (lawyers, linguists, agronomists).

## Objective
To accelerate the creation of localized AI models while simultaneously cultivating world-class open-source talent within India.

## What to Expect
- **Mentorship**: Direct collaboration with Core Maintainers and leading AI researchers.
- **Compute Access**: Generous cloud GPU credits to test and train models.
- **Impact**: Code and models you build will directly power applications across millions of rural and urban users in Bharat.
- **Stipend**: A monthly stipend to support independent researchers.

## Tracks
1. **Foundation Model Track**: Focuses on distributed training, tokenizer expansion, and optimizing inference engines.
2. **Data Engineering Track**: Focuses on large-scale parallel crawling, data purification pipelines, and synthetic data generation.
3. **Domain Expert Track**: (Non-coding) Focuses on qualitative evaluation, RLHF ranking, and guiding the behavioral tuning of the models.

## How to Apply
Applications open bi-annually. Keep an eye on our GitHub discussions and social media channels for the next cohort announcement!
