# Narayan Labs: Social Media Strategy

## 1. Mission of Narayan Labs Social Presence
The social media presence of Narayan Labs exists to **build the premier open-source AI community in India**. Our goal is to transform passive followers into active contributors by openly sharing our research, datasets, code, and the challenges we face. We aim to establish technical credibility, foster collaborative problem-solving, and champion the development of AI infrastructure customized for Bharat.

## 2. Platform Architecture

Each platform serves a distinct purpose in our contributor funnel:

### X (Twitter)
- **Role:** The Global Megaphone & Real-time Discovery.
- **Purpose:** Announce breakthroughs, drop datasets, solicit immediate feedback, and engage with the global AI/ML community (researchers, VCs, tech media).
- **Funnel Action:** Drives high-volume, top-of-funnel traffic directly to GitHub repositories and specific Discord channels.

### LinkedIn
- **Role:** Professional Credibility & Institutional Partnerships.
- **Purpose:** Connect with universities, government bodies, enterprise partners, and senior talent. Long-form posts on "Why India needs SLMs" and project milestones.
- **Funnel Action:** Drives fellowship applications, institutional collaborations, and hiring.

### GitHub
- **Role:** The Intellectual Core.
- **Purpose:** Not just code hosting, but community governance. Issues, Discussions, and PRs are treated as social interactions.
- **Funnel Action:** The ultimate destination. All other platforms exist to funnel talented individuals here to contribute code or data.

### Discord
- **Role:** The Collaborative Workshop.
- **Purpose:** Real-time coordination, troubleshooting, brainstorming, and community bonding. It’s where the "core team" interacts daily with "contributors."
- **Funnel Action:** Retains contributors who have discovered us via X/LinkedIn by giving them a sense of belonging and immediate access to maintainers.

### YouTube
- **Role:** Deep-Dive Education & Asynchronous Onboarding.
- **Purpose:** Architecture walk-throughs, paper reading groups, recorded town halls, and "How to contribute" tutorials.
- **Funnel Action:** Lowers the barrier to entry for complex repositories, turning curious developers into capable contributors.

## 3. Content Pillars

Our content is divided into five strategic pillars designed to maximize engagement and authenticity:

1. **Research Content:** Distilling complex ML concepts (e.g., SLM architecture, dataset curation strategies) into accessible threads and articles. Establishing thought leadership.
2. **Open Research Problems:** Publicly crowdsourcing solutions to hard problems we are currently facing (e.g., "How do we best tokenize low-resource Indic dialects?"). 
3. **Build-in-Public Updates:** Raw, transparent updates on our successes, failures, and infrastructure decisions. Showing the unpolished reality of building foundational models.
4. **Dataset Releases:** High-impact announcements of new, clean, open-source Indian datasets. These are our "hero" moments that drive massive engagement.
5. **Contributor Highlights:** Publicly celebrating developers and researchers who merge significant PRs or curate valuable data. Elevating our community members above the organization.

## 4. The Traffic Engine
*How social media feeds the ecosystem:*

- **X Thread** announces an Open Research Problem -> Links to a specific **GitHub Issue**.
- **LinkedIn Post** highlights a Contributor -> Links to the **GitHub PR**.
- **YouTube Video** explains the Bharat-1 Architecture -> Links to the **Discord #architecture channel** for discussion.
- **Dataset Release** everywhere -> Links to **GitHub Dataset Repo**.

*Rule of Thumb: Never post without a clear, definitive Call-To-Action (CTA) pointing to GitHub or Discord.*
