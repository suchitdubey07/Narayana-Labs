# 30-Day Content Plan

*A structured blueprint for launching Narayan Labs on social platforms and establishing immediate technical credibility.*

## Week 1: The Launch & The Mission
**Goal:** Establish presence, define the problem, and introduce the manifesto.

- **Day 1: The Launch Thread (X/LinkedIn)**
  - **Topic:** Introducing Narayan Labs.
  - **Outline:** Who we are, why India needs its own AI infrastructure, our focus on open-source Small Language Models (SLMs).
  - **CTA:** Read our Manifesto on GitHub.
- **Day 2: The Problem Statement (LinkedIn)**
  - **Topic:** Why LLMs are failing Indian languages.
  - **Outline:** The tokenization tax, lack of high-quality corpora, the need for indigenous architectures.
  - **CTA:** Join our Discord to help solve this.
- **Day 3: Video Outline (YouTube/X)**
  - **Topic:** "Building India's AI Commons."
  - **Outline:** 2-minute introductory video explaining the roadmap of Narayan Labs.
  - **CTA:** Star the GitHub repo.
- **Day 5: Friday Update (X)**
  - **Topic:** Build-in-Public Update #1.
  - **Outline:** Summary of repository initialization, first 100 stars, setting up the Discord server.
  - **CTA:** Look at our open issues for next week.

## Week 2: Datasets & Infrastructure
**Goal:** Prove technical competence and release the first valuable assets to the community.

- **Day 8: Hero Asset Drop (X/LinkedIn)**
  - **Topic:** The India AI Dataset Map.
  - **Outline:** Announcing our curated map of 120+ public Indian datasets. Why data is the real moat.
  - **CTA:** View/Fork the dataset map on GitHub.
- **Day 10: Technical Deep Dive (X)**
  - **Topic:** How we are structuring our data pipelines.
  - **Outline:** Our approach to scraping, deduplication, and quality filtering for diverse Indic languages.
  - **CTA:** Read the `data_pipeline_architecture.md` docs.
- **Day 11: Call for Contributions (LinkedIn/Discord)**
  - **Topic:** We need Data Curators.
  - **Outline:** Announcing specific domains (e.g., legal, agriculture) where we need domain experts to review datasets.
  - **CTA:** Apply for the Dataset Fellowship or join Discord.
- **Day 12: Friday Update (X)**
  - **Topic:** Build-in-Public Update #2.
  - **Outline:** Community growth stats, data scraping progress.

## Week 3: Model Architecture & Research
**Goal:** Discuss models, invite technical debate, and source engineering contributors.

- **Day 15: Research Thread (X)**
  - **Topic:** Small Language Models (SLMs) vs Large Language Models.
  - **Outline:** Why a 3B parameter model trained specifically on clean Indian data will outperform a 70B generalist model for local tasks. Compute efficiency.
  - **CTA:** Read our `slm_architecture.md`.
- **Day 17: Open Research Problem (X/GitHub)**
  - **Topic:** ORP #1: Optimizing tokenizers for Dravidian languages.
  - **Outline:** Explain the current inefficiency. Set the constraints.
  - **CTA:** Link to GitHub Issue #1. Bounty announced.
- **Day 18: Infrastructure Post (LinkedIn)**
  - **Topic:** Our Compute Strategy.
  - **Outline:** Distributed training, leveraging local GPUs, and our cloud partnerships.
  - **CTA:** Seeking compute sponsors/partners.
- **Day 19: Friday Update (X)**
  - **Topic:** Build-in-Public Update #3.
  - **Outline:** Reviewing the architectures proposed by the community for ORP #1.

## Week 4: Community & Ecosystem
**Goal:** Highlight early contributors, celebrate wins, and cement the open-source ethos.

- **Day 22: Contributor Spotlight (X/LinkedIn)**
  - **Topic:** Highlighting our first major open-source contributor.
  - **Outline:** Feature a developer who submitted a crucial PR. What they built, why it matters.
  - **CTA:** You can be next. Check our `good first issues`.
- **Day 24: Use Case Focus (LinkedIn)**
  - **Topic:** AI for Indian Agriculture.
  - **Outline:** How our upcoming models will power Kisan advisory bots. The economic impact.
  - **CTA:** Domain experts index in our Discord.
- **Day 26: Community Milestone (All Platforms)**
  - **Topic:** 30 Days of Narayan Labs.
  - **Outline:** Number of stars, contributors, Discord members, and datasets mapped.
  - **CTA:** A massive thank you. Let's build Month 2.
- **Day 27: Friday Update (X)**
  - **Topic:** Build-in-Public Update #4.
  - **Outline:** The roadmap for the next 60 days. First model training timeline.
