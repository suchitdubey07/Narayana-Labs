# Narayan Labs: Contributor Growth Strategy

## The 1,000 Contributor Goal
Building high-quality foundational models for India requires immense, distributed engineering and linguistic effort. Our goal is to scale the Narayan Labs open-source community to **1,000 active contributors** within 24 months.

We will achieve this not through traditional marketing, but by treating our open-source community as our primary product.

---

## 1. The Onboarding Funnel
To scale predictably, we need a frictionless path from "Discovering Narayan Labs on X/LinkedIn" to "Merging a First PR."

**The Path:**
1. **Awareness:** High-impact social media posts (Open Research Problems, Dataset Drops).
2. **Curiosity:** Starring the GitHub Repo / Joining Discord.
3. **Intent:** Reading the `CONTRIBUTING.md` and finding a `good first issue`.
4. **Action:** Submitting a PR or contributing a cleaned dataset.
5. **Retention:** Receiving a public shoutout, earning a Discord role, and being invited to core planning sessions.

---

## 2. Growth Drivers

### A. Open Research Problems (ORPs)
Instead of building in secret, we post our hardest technical bottlenecks publicly as ORPs on GitHub and broadcast them on social media. We attach bounties (swag, computational credits, public prestige) to the best solutions. This attracts high-tier engineers who want to prove their skills.

### B. The Dataset Challenge
Curating 100+ high-quality Indian datasets requires local context. We will host domain-specific "Dataset Challenges" (e.g., The Hindi Legal Data Sprint):
- Participants scrape, clean, or translate data in a 7-day sprint.
- Leaderboards track token volume and quality.
- Top contributors get co-authorship on the resulting dataset release paper.

### C. Narayan Labs Research Fellowships
A structured, remote, unpaid 3-month program for university students and early-career professionals.
- **Why it works:** Provides incredible resume value for participants.
- **The Deal:** Fellows commit 10 hours/week to a specific problem (e.g., Marathi tokenization). In return, they get mentorship from core maintainers and are credited in model releases.

### D. University Outreach & OSS Grants
Partnering directly with CS departments at IITs, NITs, and other major tech institutions.
- Establish "Narayan Labs Student Chapters."
- Sponsor final-year projects that align with our roadmap.
- Provide cloud compute grants to students working on Indic language modeling in our repos.

### E. Hackathons (Virtual & Physical)
Host 48-hour sprints focused on distinct challenges:
- "Fine-tune Bharat-1 to act as a Legal Assistant."
- "Build the best inference engine for a 3B parameter model on a budget Android phone."
Hackathons provide discrete, high-energy entry points for developers who might not otherwise contribute to long-term repos.

---

## 3. Recognition & Governance Systems

Contributors stay when they feel valued and see a path to influence.

- **The Leaderboard:** A public dashboard tracking PRs merged, lines of code, and tokens curated. Gamification drives engagement.
- **Discord Role Economy:** Automated progression. (e.g., Submitting 1 PR = `@Contributor`, 5 PRs = `@Maintainer-Track`, 15 PRs = `@Core-Member`).
- **Build-In-Public Shoutouts:** Every Friday, the social media engine highlights the top 3 contributors of the week across X and LinkedIn.
- **Path to Core:** A transparent, documented path showing exactly what it takes to move from a casual contributor to a Core Maintainer with commit access.
