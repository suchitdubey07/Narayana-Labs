## Tone of Communication

Narayan Labs is a serious, research-driven institution, but it is built on the radical transparency of the open-source movement. 

### 1. Research Driven (Not Hype-Driven)
- We do not use hyperbole. We do not claim AGI is imminent. We do not use excessive emojis in technical documentation.
- We deal in benchmarks, token counts, architectural trade-offs, and empirical results.
- **Example (Bad):** "Our new model will literally change the world and disrupt EVERYTHING! 🔥🚀🤖"
- **Example (Good):** "Bharat-1 achieves state-of-the-art results on the IndicGLUE benchmark for 3B parameter models, trained specifically on high-quality vernacular data."

### 2. Open Source Focused (Not Corporate)
- We communicate like peers to developers. We share our struggles, our bugs, and our failed experiments openly.
- We use words like "Community," "Contributors," and "Ecosystem" instead of "Users" or "Customers".
- **Example (Bad):** "We are proud to announce the launch of our proprietary enterprise solution."
- **Example (Good):** "We're open-sourcing our entire pre-training corpus today. Have at it, break things, and PR your fixes."

### 3. Technical Credibility (Not Simplified)
- We assume our audience is intelligent. We don't dumb down the math or the architecture.
- For broad platforms (LinkedIn), we contextualize *why* the tech matters, but we still link to the hard technical specs.
- We value clear, concise, Markdown-formatted communication.

### 4. Community Collaboration (Not Isolated)
- We use "We" to refer to the entire community, not just the founding team.
- We actively push credit down to contributors rather than hoarding it at the lab level.
- **Example (Bad):** "I built this amazing new data pipeline."
- **Example (Good):** "Thanks to a massive effort from 14 community contributors, our new data pipeline is live."

## Visual Identity (Draft Concepts)

*Note: Final hex codes and logo SVGs will be added here once designed.*

### Aesthetic Keywords:
- **Clean / Minimalist** (Reflecting optimized, small models)
- **Deep / Subdued** (Dark modes, glassmorphism UI)
- **Rooted** (Subtle nods to Indian heritage without being stereotypical or overt)

### Color Palette Concept:
- **Primary Background:** Deep Slate/Obsidian (Hex: #0B0E14)
- **Primary Accent:** Neon Saffron / Deep Orange (Hex: #FF6A00) – For buttons, critical links, terminal status indicators.
- **Secondary Accent:** Muted Gold or Electric Indigo.
- **Text:** High-contrast off-white (Hex: #F2F2F2) for readability against dark backgrounds.

### Typography:
- **Headings:** A modern, bold sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Manrope*, or *Satoshi*).
- **Body & Code:** A highly legible monospaced or clean sans font (e.g., *JetBrains Mono* for code, *Roboto* for text).
