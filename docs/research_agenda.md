# Narayan Labs Research Agenda

Our initial focus revolves around 10 critical sectors where AI can drive massive socio-economic impact in India.

## Program 1: Indian Legal AI Model
Developing SLMs trained on Supreme Court and High Court judgments to assist citizens and lawyers in legal research and drafting.

## Program 2: Agricultural Advisory Model
Multilingual models delivering hyper-local crop advisories, pest management strategies, and weather integration for farmers.

## Program 3: MSME Credit Intelligence Model
Analyzing diverse financial records and alternative data to provide conversational insights for loan officers and business owners.

## Program 4: Multilingual Education Tutor
Personalized tutoring models designed to bridge foundational literacy and numeracy gaps in deeply rural dialects.

## Program 5: Policy Interpretation Model
Simplifying complex government schemes, policies, and eligibility criteria into conversational formats for citizens.

## Program 6: Financial Regulation Assistant
Assisting compliance officers in navigating SEBI, RBI, and IRDAI regulations dynamically and accurately.

## Program 7: Parliamentary Debate Analyzer
Models capable of analyzing transcripts from the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha to identify legislative trends, sentiment, and policy shifts.

## Program 8: Healthcare Advisory Assistant
A secure, verifiable model providing primary health triage advice and regional preventative care guidelines (not replacing doctors, but augmenting ASHA workers).

## Program 9: Rural Governance Assistant
Assisting Gram Panchayats and local administrators in drafting proposals, understanding block-level data, and tracking developmental indicators.

## Program 10: Economic Research AI
A tool for economists and journalists to query macroeconomic datasets (e.g., NSSO, Census) through natural language interfaces.
