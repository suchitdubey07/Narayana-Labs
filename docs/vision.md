# Narayan Labs Vision

Our vision is a self-reliant AI ecosystem in Bharat that is open, inclusive, and tailored to the unique linguistic and cultural nuances of the subcontinent.

## Core Pillars
1. **Localization**: Deep support for Indic languages, prioritizing low-resource dialects.
2. **Efficiency**: Pushing the boundaries of what Small Language Models can achieve.
3. **Application**: Grounding our research in high-impact domains like agriculture, law, finance, and education.
4. **Community**: Fostering the next generation of AI researchers and developers in India.
