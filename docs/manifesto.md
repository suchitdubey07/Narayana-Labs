# Narayan Labs Manifesto

## The Philosophy of Narayan Labs
We believe that Artificial Intelligence must be accessible, open, and equitable. We reject the paradigm where advanced capabilities are locked behind corporate APIs and restricted to those who can afford massive compute infrastructure.

Our core philosophy is rooted in self-reliance (Atmanirbharta). We are building an open AI infrastructure for Bharat, created by the community, for the community.

## Why India Needs Open AI Infrastructure
India is culturally, linguistically, and economically diverse. The generalized large models developed primarily in the West often fail to capture the nuances of our languages or address our unique socio-economic challenges.
To build a thriving ecosystem, we need localized models, trained on authentic open datasets, and evaluated against culturally relevant benchmarks.

## The Power of Small Language Models (SLMs)
Massive models with billions of parameters are undeniably powerful, but their operational costs restrict access. We champion Small Language Models because:
1. They lower the barrier to entry, allowing startups and researchers to experiment locally.
2. They are more easily adapted to specialized tasks where inference latency and cost are critical.
3. They function effectively on edge hardware, connecting even rural populations to state-of-the-art AI.

## Our Commitment
We are committed to:
- **Open Research**: Publishing not just our successes, but our failures and learnings.
- **Open Datasets**: Developing robust data pipelines to build representative, high-quality, and permissively licensed datasets.
- **Open Weights**: Ensuring that the weights of our foundational models remain freely accessible forever.
- **Open Governance**: Operating as a community-driven institution where contributors shape our roadmap and priorities.
