# Narayan Labs - 3-Year Roadmap

This document outlines the strategic vision and tangible milestones for Narayan Labs over the next three years. Let's build AI for Bharat together.

## Year 1: Foundations (Dataset, First Model & Community)
**Objective:** Establish the foundational open AI infrastructure and train our first robust baseline SLM tailored for Indian languages.

* **Q1-Q2:**
  * Define data scraping pipelines and labeling methods.
  * Curate and release high-quality, open-source localized datasets across 5+ regional languages.
  * Establish governance and community guidelines.

* **Q3:**
  * Complete infrastructure setup and distributed training pipelines.
  * Begin pre-training phase of our first foundational Small Language Model (SLM) on multi-node GPU clusters.

* **Q4:**
  * Train and release the first foundational SLM for Bharat.
  * Launch the Narayan Labs Fellowship Program.
  * Build a strong core contributor community numbering 100+ active researchers and developers.

## Year 2: Domain Specialization (Applied AI)
**Objective:** Fine-tune foundational models to solve real-world problems in critical sectors and enhance our dataset repository.

* **Q1-Q2:**
  * Release specialized domain-specific models (Agriculture AI, Legal AI, Finance AI).
  * Expand datasets to include highly specialized corpora (e.g., Supreme Court judgments, rural agricultural advisories, MSME financial profiles).

* **Q3-Q4:**
  * Publish peer-reviewed research papers on efficient fine-tuning techniques for low-resource Indian languages.
  * Release additional domain models (Education Tutor, Healthcare Advisory).
  * Host the first annual "AI for Bharat" developer conference.

## Year 3: Scale & Global Ecosystem
**Objective:** Scale Narayan Labs into a globally recognized open AI research ecosystem with extensive localized impact.

* **Q1-Q2:**
  * Achieve broad adoption of Narayan Labs models in government and enterprise applications across India.
  * Collaborate with global open-source AI organizations to standardize evaluation metrics for Indian languages.

* **Q3-Q4:**
  * Expand research agenda into multimodal models (Voice and Vision) tailored for Indian contexts.
  * Scale the contributor community to thousands of active members globally.
  * Solidify Narayan Labs as the premier hub for open AI research and infrastructure in the Global South.
